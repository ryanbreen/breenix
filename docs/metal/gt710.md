# GT 710: firmware framebuffer and console (E19, milestones 1–3)

The first three milestones adopt the framebuffer the firmware handed to the kernel.
They require no NVIDIA register programming, modeset or command submission. The
kernel reports the handed-off mode as width, height, pixel format and pitch, and
reports the buffer's physical base and mapped byte length. The bootloader API does
not carry the firmware's numeric GOP mode index.

`metal-gt710/firmware` measures the `gt710` suite's `firmware` category. Its mode case
requires a nonempty mode whose pitch and height fit the mapped byte length. Its
owner case requires the entire mapping in a prefetchable memory BAR belonging to
PCI 10de:128b, with matching function and BAR facts in the PCI census. The embedded
display remaining on the bus cannot satisfy it.

`metal-gt710/device` measures `device`: the 10de:128b function, subsystem 1462:8c93 and
interrupt line; its BAR list and PCIe link; and matching display-class discovery
and firmware framebuffer attachment. `PCI_DISPLAY_LINK` reports the raw Link
Capabilities and Link Status registers and their speed codes and lane widths.
The decoding is from PCI Express Base Specification 4.0 section 7.5.3, offsets 0x0c and
0x12 in the PCI Express capability: speed bits 3:0, width bits 9:4. Speed codes
1 and 2 mean 2.5 and 5 GT/s. The negotiated link is reported, not inferred from
the slot's generation or width.

The host-run cases consume a completed boot's retained capture. They require a
clean successful capture, no SOL gap, and the requested kernel and tools commit.
They recompute their verdict from the captured facts when the archive is scored.
On the boot host, after the boot ends:

```sh
python3 ~/.local/share/breenix-metal/gt710-suite --boot ID --sha SHA
```

The records are `gt710-firmware.json` and `gt710-device.json` in that boot's private
store. The runner updates its milestone verdict and finished sequence without
booting again. Raw captures stay private.

`metal-gt710/console` measures the host-run `gt710/console` acceptance case. It
requires the same capture to pass both GT 710 reporting categories, screen/font
read-back and the complete E16 suite (including memory reclamation): `screen`,
`emulation`, `vt-devices`, `vt-switch`, `scrollback`, `chvt`, `getty` and `keys`.
The acceptance sequence at the final head is a console-suite boot, a console-mode
SOL challenge with screen read-back, and the persistent install's default init
reaching its login prompt. Both reporting categories are also checked on the
console and persistent captures. The persistent install is restored to origin/main
after the lane's acceptance sequence. This scope claims no accelerated rendering or modesetting.
