## Schema and choices

`libs/libuitest/src/schema.rs` is compiled into libuitest and directly into
`tests/suite_manifests.rs`. It validates field types and integer ranges,
reference metadata, unique named regions, masks with reasons, tolerance,
registered typed formula signatures and the five structural predicates in
`docs/gui/plan.md` section 7.5. Nested unknown fields and unknown versions are
refused, including `edgeRadius`. The optional `frameChecks` key uses the same
strict extension pattern as `aggregate` under U8; other unknown keys remain
errors. Host loading also checks case membership and duplicate check IDs.

Choices from section 7.5, also recorded in the change's commit message:

- A tolerance reason is inside the tolerance object. Screen and surface source
  objects carry their kind; a window source also requires its window key.
- Rectangles use checked u32 coordinates and endpoints. A manifest has no
  captured extent, so host loading defers that bound. Guest validation accepts
  the captured extent and checks it before comparison.
- Mask subtraction produces disjoint rectangles. Overlapping masks count once;
  a mask applies to each enclosing region, matching libpixcheck's rule.
  Region coverage and effective-domain coverage use integer arithmetic.
- An outside rectangle has a positive interior margin on each side. Its domain
  must retain at least one pixel and 75% of the geometric outside area.
- Equal-region domains count pairs for which both pixels survive masks, with
  matching sizes, disjoint geometry, at least two pairs and 75% pair coverage.
- A previous structural check contributes its actual compared pixels, including
  both operands' valid pairs for equality. Difference thresholds use the
  intersection with that preceding domain, in the same case and source.
- Formula signatures are suite-source data supplied to the validator. The
  `schema-pattern` fixture signature exercises bounded u32/i32, colour, rect,
  window-key and u64 seed types. Reference generation remains a later unit.

The parser dependency is serde_json 1.0.154 with alloc, under its MIT option
(the crate declares MIT OR Apache-2.0; LICENSE-MIT was inspected). Geometry
was designed from section 7.5 and rectangle subtraction, without adapting
external implementation code. No driver interface was added.

## Check first and results

Check-only commit `63efb11a` adds the fixture table and replaces six pending
bodies: unknown-field, mask, mask-cap, formula-params, schema-version and
structural-vacuous. It precedes schema.rs. The new admission test compiles on
the old validator and fails at the intended assertion: a valid `frameChecks`
entry is rejected as an unexpected key. The initial test setup also exposed
existing `collects` refusals; a minimal valid manifest isolates schema admission
without changing those existing assertions or inputs.

The final table has 125 definitions. It includes the exact 75% region cutoff,
overlapping mask unions, four one-pixel border strips leaving 9,604/10,000
pixels, outside and equal-pair coverage boundaries, typed formula refusals and
structural domains after masks. Predecessor fixture IDs were made distinct
within a case. Additional fixtures strengthened coverage; no refusal was
removed or changed into an acceptance to pass the implementation.

The draft accepted a difference threshold of 397 after an outside check that
compared only its 396 border pixels. The added deterministic fixture fails on
that draft, then passes after limiting the previous footprint. Equal-region
predecessor fixtures also cover the second operand and its surviving pairs.

`frame_checks_are_optional_and_strict` passes after the implementation.
`frame_schema_guest_and_host_agree_over_fixtures` passes with identical
acceptance/refusal messages over 125/125 definitions. It executes the six real
guest case functions on the host. The structural case additionally invokes
libpixcheck on a clean outside region and on a planted forbidden border pixel;
the clean region passes and the planted pixel fails.

The existing `suite_manifests_match_their_binaries` check fails with 13
manifest/source diagnostics. A clean detached origin/main `a8371640` reproduces
the exact same diagnostic text: setup proof refusals in BreenGL, console, GPU
and ui-testing; seven GT710 hostRunner placement errors; and two ui-testing
`collects` key refusals. Existing manifest verdicts are therefore unchanged,
but the global check is not claimed clean. The inherited parser/catalog
follow-up is #2748. Its checks and the manifest inputs are untouched. The
private baseline checkout was removed after comparison.
