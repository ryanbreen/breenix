# #789 slice 2 — scheduler-lock IRQ shape

Slice 2 repairs the scheduler-lock producer identified by ruling R153 and adds
a structural ratchet for the whole acquisition surface. The tracked defect is
#790.

## What changed

`Scheduler::is_current_idle_thread` retains its `Option<bool>` contract and
non-blocking `try_lock`, but now masks local interrupts before acquisition and
restores the prior state after the guard is dropped. `try_schedule` had no live
caller on `origin/main`; it and its `#[allow(dead_code)]` annotation were
deleted.

The new census also found three acquisitions that were outside a local mask and
could not be derived from an interrupt or exception entry: `init`,
`init_with_current`, and the currently callerless `try_liveness_snapshot`.
Their complete guard lifetimes now use the same arch-neutral mask.

The masked choice is deliberately conservative. AArch64 has a lock-free
`CPU_IS_IDLE` scheduling flag, but x86_64 does not share that representation,
and the flag is not the authoritative current-thread/idle-thread identity pair
used by `Scheduler::refuse_idle_block`. Extending lock-free identity publication
would require auditing the context-switch publication sites. In contrast,
`arch_without_interrupts` already saves and restores IF on x86_64 and DAIF on
AArch64. Masking before acquisition prevents same-CPU IRQ re-entry for the
entire held window, while the scheduler lock continues to serialize peer CPUs;
restoring the saved state also makes nested use correct when interrupts were
already masked.

## Structural ratchet

`tests/teardown_structure.rs::scheduler_lock_acquisitions_are_irq_safe_by_shape`
censuses every Rust function below `kernel/src` whose body directly calls raw
`SCHEDULER.lock()` or `SCHEDULER.try_lock()`, then derives the scheduler helper
spellings from those raw sites and censuses all callers.
claim-lint:ok: `tests/teardown_structure.rs::scheduler_lock_acquisitions_are_irq_safe_by_shape`
and its origin/main mutation run recorded below. A site is admitted by shape
only when it is:

- lexically inside `without_interrupts` or `arch_without_interrupts`;
- after an explicit architectural interrupt disable with no later enable;
- in the false branch of a value read from `are_enabled`; or
- reached only through callers that satisfy one of those shapes or bottom out
  at an interrupt/exception entry symbol derived from the AArch64 vector table
  or x86 IDT and assembly entry stubs.

There is no function-name allowlist. The raw primitives and their callers are
derived from syntax, and an empty primitive census fails rather than passing
vacuously.

## Anti-vacuity

The pre-fix worktree was detached at `origin/main` commit `d6b7a186`; only the
two ratchet commits were applied with `git cherry-pick --no-commit`, leaving the
scheduler source at the pre-fix revision.

Pre-fix invocation, verbatim:

```text
CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR=/tmp/breenix-789-prefx-worktree rustc --edition=2021 --test /tmp/breenix-789-prefx-worktree/tests/teardown_structure.rs -o /tmp/breenix-789-teardown-prefx && /tmp/breenix-789-teardown-prefx scheduler_lock_acquisitions_are_irq_safe_by_shape --nocapture
```

Exit: 101. It printed these seven failing function identities:

```text
kernel/src/task/scheduler.rs::init
kernel/src/task/scheduler.rs::init_with_current
kernel/src/task/scheduler.rs::is_current_idle_thread
```

Thus the required two R153 producers, `is_current_idle_thread` and
`try_schedule`, are both observed by the pre-fix test. The raw helper failures

x86 userspace builder, which requires the absent `rust-fork/library`. Compiling
the std-only integration test directly with `rustc --test` is the host-test
equivalent and runs the same `tests/teardown_structure.rs` test binary.

## Scope of the claim

Slice 2 does not close #789 by itself. The parked branch's nine
`idle_sleep.rs` call sites are slice 3 and are not present on `origin/main`.
This slice repairs the latent producer and prevents the same acquisition shape
from returning.

The `head_5` observation—heartbeats remaining alive while one service chain
stalled—can represent a second defect. Slice 2 does not explain, repair, or
otherwise change that shape. The IRQ path and soft-lockup watchdog are
untouched, as are the 5 of 5 Tier-1 prohibited files.
