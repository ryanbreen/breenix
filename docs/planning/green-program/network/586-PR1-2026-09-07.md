## The defect and the repair

`kernel/src/test_framework/registry.rs` before this commit selected the
classification from the delivery facts alone. The three arms read
`queue_depth` (sampled at `:2942` at the parent commit) and
`client_has_data` (`:2943`); `reader_state` was sampled two lines later, was
printed in the diagnostic, and 0 of the 3 arms consulted it.

After this commit the reader fact is derived at `registry.rs:2979` from
`reader_state`, and the selector at `:2985` matches on
`(queue_depth > 0, client_has_data, reader_fact)`. `wake_ms == 0` at `:3024`
means "the reader had not run", which is not the same fact as "the reader was
not woken", and only `reader_state` can answer the second.

## The ratchet

`tests/loopback_pump_structure.rs:2014`
`validate_loopback_wake_classification_reads_reader_state` asserts, over
`kernel/src/test_framework/registry.rs`:

1. a reader fact is derived before the selector, and its derivation reads
   `reader_state` and `is_blocked`;
2. the selector reads `queue_depth`, `client_has_data` and the reader fact;
3. the selector has at least 4 arms and no two arms share their text.

It is a shape check, not a list of words: the arms may be re-worded and cases
may be added. The arm census is taken from `code_mask`, so it counts literal
runs rather than matching any string.

Two delete-mutation legs, `tests/loopback_pump_structure.rs:2097`:

* drop the reader fact from the selector scrutinee (the 586 defect exactly)
  gives a red validator;
* drop `reader_state` from the derivation of the fact gives a red validator.

The leg first asserts the unmutated source passes, so neither mutation is
vacuous.

## Fix pass — review finding closed (2026-09-07)

This fix pass closes one finding from the review of this round.

**V-4 (minor/code)** — `tests/loopback_pump_structure.rs:2014` (as it stood
at commit `64502e39`), `validate_loopback_wake_classification_reads_reader_state`.
The ratchet's 4 shape checks (arm count >= 4, arm-text distinctness, the
reader fact derived from `reader_state` and `is_blocked`, the selector

the #586 misreport by pasting its exact string into a different arm's cell:
replacing `registry.rs:3002`'s
`"delivery landed and the recv waiter left Blocked but had not run by the
deadline"` (the `(false, true, ReaderFact::LeftBlocked)` arm) with the old,
pre-PR1 defect text `"delivery landed but the recv waiter was never
woken"`, then running
`bash scripts/run-structure-tests.sh loopback_pump_structure
loopback_wake_classification_reads_reader_state`, returned `test result: ok.
1 passed; 0 failed`. 0 of the 4 shape checks moved, so 0 of the 4 caught it.
The validator's own doc comment says "the arms may be re-worded freely"
(`tests/loopback_pump_structure.rs:2007` at `64502e39`, unchanged by this
fix pass) -- that sentence says arms may be reworded, not that a specific,
already-known-false string may return; the review's own citation of this
line as `586-PR1:136` was the round doc's paraphrase of the same shape
check, not this exact wording, which lives in the source comment. Neither

return unratcheted.

### The repair

`tests/loopback_pump_structure.rs`'s
`validate_loopback_wake_classification_reads_reader_state` now carries a
fifth, content-level check, run after the 4 shape checks and before the
function returns `Ok(())`: an arm's text may not contain the literal
substring `"never woken"`. This is deliberately not scoped to the one cell
the review moved the phrase into -- the check runs over 12 of 12 arms,
because 0 of the selector's 3 inputs (`queue_depth`, `client_has_data`,
`reader_fact`) is capable of witnessing whether a wake happened;
`reader_fact` only observes where the reader sits *now*
(`StillBlocked`/`LeftBlocked`/`Terminated`/`NoRow`), which is a different
fact than whether a wake call ever ran. 0 of 3 facts can prove the phrase in
any of the 12 cells, so the check applies uniformly to all 12 rather than
naming the one cell the reproduction happened to hit.

A new test,
`loopback_wake_classification_validator_rejects_the_586_misreport_phrase`
(`tests/loopback_pump_structure.rs:2157`), reproduces the review's exact
mutation byte-for-byte (the same `LeftBlocked`-arm replacement, the same old
string) and asserts the validator now returns `Err`.
