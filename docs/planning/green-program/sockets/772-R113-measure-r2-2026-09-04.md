## Partition check

Per boot, per reason (6 reasons): `DISPATCH_NOPROGRESS_SAVE_<reason>` ==
`DISPATCH_NOPROGRESS_REVISIT_<reason>` + `..._ZERO_ITER_<reason>` +
`..._ITERS_UNKNOWN_<reason>`. Plus, per boot, 3 aggregate checks: the
whole-machine `DISPATCH_NOPROGRESS_REVISIT_CPU0` /
`..._ZERO_ITER_CPU0` / `..._ITERS_UNKNOWN_CPU0` each equal the sum of that
same field across the 6 reasons. 9 checks/boot × 6 boots = 54 checks.

**54 of 54 checks hold, 0 violations**, computed independently in this slot
from the 6 committed `census.json` files (recomputation script:
`python3` one-liner over the JSON `counters` dict, not committed —
`docs/planning/green-program/sockets/serials/772-r113-r2/` holds only the
raw driver output).

Note: `DISPATCH_NO_PROGRESS_CPU0` (column 4 of the per-boot table) is **not**
part of this partition and is not equal to `REVISIT + ZERO_ITER +
ITERS_UNKNOWN` in any of the 6 boots (e.g. L1 boot_01: `DISPATCH_NO_PROGRESS`
= 448, `REVISIT + ZERO_ITER + ITERS_UNKNOWN` = 141 + 625 + 0 = 766) — the two
are counters over different populations; there is no `DISPATCH_NOPROGRESS_SAVE`
symbol without a reason suffix to check `DISPATCH_NO_PROGRESS_CPU0` against
directly. Each boot's own `counter_symbols.txt` (e.g.
`docs/planning/green-program/sockets/serials/772-r113-r2/L1/boot_01/counter_symbols.txt`)
lists the 41 `DISPATCH_*`/`WAIT_LOOP_PARK_*` symbols this build's ELF
carries — 41 on both arms, 6 of 6 boots, against 39 on 6 of 6 round-1 boots,
which carried no `WAIT_LOOP_PARK_*` statics; no aggregate-SAVE symbol among
them.
