# Issue 855 scheduler sampling and buffer follow-up

The buffer follow-up passed the requested 1500 ms target in three boots.
The sample-only evidence below remains **failed** historical evidence;
the existing strict gate floor remains 1000 ms. Neither was lowered.

`TRACE_DIAG_SAMPLE` remains 1024. The new aarch64-only
`TRACE_SCHED_DIAG_SAMPLE` is now settled at 8192, with a per-CPU relaxed call counter and
`SCHED_DIAG_SAMPLE_DROPPED` call-level accounting. The existing three ring
writes and the x86 no-op stub remain intact. The scheduler also calls the
helper from wake-timer attribution sites; sampling applies to those calls.

## Measurements and stopping decision

The first factor, 8192, produced spans of 921, 895, and 901 ms; strict x3
exited 1. Doubling to 16384 produced 896, 938, and 936 ms; strict x3 exited 1.
Raw serials are under [serials/855](serials/855), named
`aarch64-sched8192-failed-boot-{1,2,3}.txt` and
`aarch64-sched16384-failed-boot-{1,2,3}.txt`. Both are historical
plateau measurements, not accepted passing evidence. The older `after` and
`final-boot` fixtures remain as failed evidence of the discovery that
SCHED_DIAG occupied 960–1022 of 1024 slots on CPUs 1–3.

A hardware GDB breakpoint at `kernel::capture::emit` on the factor-16384
feature build found the following counts per 1024-slot peer ring:

| CPU | SCHED_DIAG total | CTX_SWITCH_ENTRY + SCHED_QUEUE_STATE | SCHED_RESCHED_IPI_RECV |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 | 772 | 196 |
| 2 | 9 | 722 | 248 |
| 3 | 6 | 784 | 192 |

The [decoded snapshot](serials/855/aarch64-gdb-ring-mix-sched16384.txt) and
[binary dump](serials/855/aarch64-gdb-rings-sched16384.bin) preserve that
probe separately from strict-gate measurements. The snapshot spans are
969, 988, and 878 ms for those peers. The dump uses 16448-byte CPU strides,
1024 `<QHBBI` entries, and a 62500000 Hz counter; the write index is the
64-bit value at offset 16384 of each CPU buffer.

Inference: further SCHED_DIAG-only sampling cannot plausibly bridge the
roughly 600 ms shortfall when that family occupies only 6–9 slots. Tuning
stopped after this first doubling, based on the measured plateau and GDB,
instead of trying another factor without a supporting mechanism. The user ruled that those non-diagnostic events must remain unsampled.
The buffer follow-up therefore increases the shared capacity to 2048,
without another sampling-factor increase.

## Buffer follow-up decision

Sampling began with two diagnostic families (CTX_DIAG and DEFER_REQUEUE) at
256, then increased to 1024; GDB identified a third dominant family,
SCHED_DIAG, which was sampled up to 8192. The 8192 spans were 921/895/901 ms;
16384 returned 896/938/936 ms, with only 6–9 SCHED_DIAG events in each
1024-slot peer ring. Sampling alone had plateaued against legitimate,
non-diagnostic scheduler traffic. Keep 8192 because 16384 gave no material
gain, and close the remaining gap by measuring a 2x buffer increase to 2048
entries. CTX_SWITCH_ENTRY, SCHED_QUEUE_STATE, and reschedule IPI events stay
unsampled; the earlier failing serial fixtures remain attributed evidence.

Issue [#855](https://github.com/ryanbreen/breenix/issues/855), Option 1, says:
“1024 -> 4096 costs 16 CPUs x 64 KiB = 1 MiB of `.bss` and buys 4x with no
information loss”. That describes the proposed total entry storage at 4096,
not the incremental cost. This round first measures the 2x step:
`(2048 - 1024) * 16 bytes/entry * 16 MAX_CPUS = 262144 bytes = 256 KiB`
additional `.bss` on **each** architecture. Alignment and metadata sizes do
not change: each aligned CPU buffer grows from 16448 to 32832 bytes, so the
16-buffer array grows from 263168 to 525312 bytes.

The capacity assertion now computes from TRACE_BUFFER_SIZE. The structural
ratchet pins literal 2048 and its 2048-to-1024 mutation must panic; the two
diagnostic factors are pinned at 1024 and 8192. PR-2's ring-depth assertions
are untouched. Current documentation was refreshed; historical serials and
snapshot offsets still describe their original 1024-entry builds.

## Buffer follow-up validation

Accepted final values: TRACE_DIAG_SAMPLE=1024, TRACE_SCHED_DIAG_SAMPLE=8192,
TRACE_BUFFER_SIZE=2048. Strict x3 exited 0; 55/55 structure suites passed (824 tests).
The three spans were 1801, 1638, and 1793 ms: margins of 301, 138, and 293 ms
above 1500. Stop at 2048; no 4096 probe or further sampling increase is needed.
Accepted raw serials, with provenance headers, are
[boot 1](serials/855/aarch64-buffer2048-after-boot-1.txt),
