Publish the CPU0 softirq daemon at the aarch64 init handoff

The pinned CPU0 worker cannot run while kernel_main retains its boot
preemption pin. Its early Ready publication tripped the existing strand
census in three strict boots. Defer that publication until the pin is
released with interrupts still masked before the init ERET. Keep the
existing census assertion and add a publication-order ratchet.
